About these mockups

  • Checklist tasks, workbook prompts, the first-year plan, and the IDP worksheet come from the OPA orientation workbook.
  • People, events, and numbers are sample content. “Today” is fixed at Oct 11, 2026.
  • Cardo and Source Sans 3 stand in for the Yale typeface and Mallory, which only load on YaleSites.
  • The “Yale University” signature and footer logo are text stand-ins. The real app should use the official files from the University Printer.
  • Nothing here is saved. Reloading the page resets the sample data.
